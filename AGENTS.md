# Project Instructions

## Devpost compliance is mandatory

Before planning, editing, reviewing, testing, deploying, recording a demo, or preparing a submission, read `DEVPOST_REQUIREMENTS.md` and `.devpost-hackathon-state.json`.

For every meaningful change:

1. Identify the affected requirements and judging criteria before implementation.
2. Keep scope compatible with the submission deadline and prioritize a complete, working WebMCP flow.
3. Verify the change with evidence appropriate to the affected checklist items.
4. Report any compliance gap immediately; never silently waive or reinterpret a requirement.
5. Append a dated entry to `DEVPOST_COMPLIANCE_LOG.md` when implementation work begins.

Do not mark rules as acknowledged unless `.devpost-hackathon-state.json` has `rules_acknowledged: true`. Only the participant's exact `yes` response to the official rules review may unlock that state.

Before any submission or consequential decision, refresh the relevant live requirements through the Devpost Hackathons plugin because dates, announcements, fields, and rules may change. The official Devpost website and live Devpost data always prevail over local files.

After 2026-09-03 20:00 UTC / 2026-09-04 03:00 Asia/Bangkok, stop before modifying the submitted Devpost entry, repo, live site, video, or team. Continued development must use a separate fork until winners are announced.

The participant must choose the final project name. AI may brainstorm problem directions and naming criteria, but must not select or present an AI-generated name as final.
